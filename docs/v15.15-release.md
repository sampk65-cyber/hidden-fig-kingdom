# V15.15 — Recorded musical beat grids

Fruit in all 11 recorded tracks now follows a repeating musical beat map instead of selecting the loudest unrelated attacks. Easy selects every other measured main beat, medium adds the remaining main beats, and hard adds supported half-beat attacks. Every lower difficulty target retains its time and lane at higher difficulty. Stage flashes use the same per-track pulse map, including the initial phase and local tempo variation.

The player separates render-ahead source scheduling from audible device time. Drawing and judgments share the compensated audible clock; getOutputTimestamp is validated before use, with latency-property fallback and the rhythm compressor's fixed6ms lookahead. Pause/resume retains the audible position. Sources still use currentTime scheduling.

## Analysis and scope

- `analyze-rhythm-grid.py` decodes the committed MP3s with FFmpeg, computes low/mid/high spectral attacks and tracks a regular pulse with dynamic programming. `--scan-tempo` reproduces quarter/eighth phase-coherence candidate rankings; reviewed tempo priors resolve octave ambiguity.
- Targets require measured acoustic attacks near the beat or half beat. Weak or silent slots remain empty. Tempo priors are130,94,100,145,82,103,95.75,128,96,88,132 BPM in catalog order; actual pulse intervals adapt to the recording.
- `rhythm-grid-analysis.json` records source hashes, note counts and internal alignment with the measured pulse map. These percentages are not an independent musical-accuracy score or a full written-score transcription.
- All11 MP3s are byte-unchanged. The seven synthesized songs, arrangements and21 charts are identical toV15.14. All source outside the rhythm implementation is byte-identical after normalizing the two version labels. Greenhouse, scores/rewards, Firebase, weather and other games are preserved.
